# Final video: visible action sequence

Actual public-app actions with visible cursor and click markers. Normal speech and playback speed. Synthetic data only.

## 1. Supply Dispatcher
0.00-11.74 seconds

A delayed supplier puts an order at risk. Watch the complete recovery workflow on the deployed Vultr application, from entering the task to confirming the saved replacement.

## 2. 01 / Define the delayed order
11.74-33.02 seconds

First, enter the order and product. Set the required quantity, maximum budget, and delivery deadline. Confirm the supplier delay. Here, twelve units must arrive within five days, with a budget of fifteen hundred. These become mandatory conditions for every alternative.

## 3. 02 / Enter supplier alternatives
33.02-54.86 seconds

Next, enter the supplier offers under those same conditions. Supplier A offers twelve units for twelve hundred in four days. Supplier B is faster but costs more. The cheaper third offer arrives in eight days, which misses the deadline. Now the comparison is ready.

## 4. 03 / Analyze disruption
54.86-76.14 seconds

Click Analyze disruption. Supply Dispatcher calculates the best admissible supplier and independently verifies the result. Vultr Serverless Inference turns the checked selection into a clear business explanation. The buyer receives a consistent recommendation, with the reasoning and supporting comparison ready for review.

## 5. 04 / Review the recommendation
76.14-96.58 seconds

Review the comparison before acting. Switch from the table to metrics and charts. The cheapest offer fails the deadline. Supplier A is the lowest cost admissible option, within budget and available quantity. The recommendation shows its cost and delivery time alongside the explanation.

## 6. 05 / Approve and inspect evidence
96.58-117.66 seconds

Click Approve replacement to confirm the choice. Supply Dispatcher saves the supplier, cost, and delivery time, then independently reads back the committed result. Open Evidence to inspect the completed checks. The green confirmation gives the buyer a verified record of the successful order recovery.

## 7. 06 / Reuse and safe repetition
117.66-138.94 seconds

Edit conditions returns to the input form. Run the same case again. Verified experience can avoid a repeated model explanation, while calculation and checking still run. Approving the same saved decision again is safe: the order was already updated, so no duplicate update is created.

## 8. Reliable execution
138.94-161.31 seconds

Supply Dispatcher keeps calculation isolated so the workflow stays responsive. Here, an intentionally looping task is stopped automatically. Its container is cleaned up, the host continues working, and the next calculation succeeds. This automatic recovery keeps a failed task from interrupting subsequent work.

## 9. Supply Dispatcher online
161.31-177.39 seconds

Supply Dispatcher brings supplier comparison, clear explanations, buyer approval, and verified order recovery into one workflow. Open the application to try it. Public source and setup instructions are available on GitHub.
